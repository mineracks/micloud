Step 1 of 5

Sign in to the iCloud you want to protect

This Mac quietly becomes your private mirror. Sign in with your Apple ID — micloud never sees your password. It only watches what iCloud already brings to this Mac, and copies it somewhere you own.

Waiting for your Apple ID…
You'll approve a code on your iPhone — that's normal and expected.
Two-factor is a good thing — it means only you can start a backup. Approve the prompt on your phone and come back.
Step 2 of 5

Set up your Photos the light way

We'll make a fresh photo library on this Mac and turn on Optimise Storage. That keeps only a small working copy here — your full library stays in iCloud and streams to your own storage as it changes. Nothing is ever downloaded twice.

Thin cache, full protection
A 200 GB library needs only a few GB on this Mac.
Optimise Storage
We'll walk you through the two clicks in the Photos app — it takes about a minute.

Where should your copy live?
