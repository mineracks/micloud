# Security

micloud handles the most personal data a person has, and it runs on a Mac that is signed into
their Apple account. If you find a weakness in how it stores credentials, talks to Nextcloud or
Immich, or handles the data it mirrors, please report it privately.

- Email: info@mineracks.com with "micloud security" in the subject.
- Please include the version or commit, the platform, and steps to reproduce.
- We will acknowledge within three business days and keep you informed until it is resolved.

Please do not open a public issue for a vulnerability until a fix is available.
